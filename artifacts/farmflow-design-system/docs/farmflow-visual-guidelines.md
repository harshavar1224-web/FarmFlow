# FarmFlow visual guidelines

FarmFlow is a demand-led local food network. The interface should make the link
between consumer demand, pre-orders, harvest planning, fresh local supply, and
delivery understandable at a glance.

## Color

- Use deep forest green for primary actions, links, and positive or verified
  states.
- Keep backgrounds warm and light in the default theme; the dark theme uses
  forest-tinted surfaces rather than neutral black.
- Reserve muted harvest gold for selective highlights.
- Avoid excessive bright green and overly colorful agricultural treatments.
- Keep data series distinct and on-brand so supply, demand, surplus, logistics,
  and impact can be compared without relying on text alone.

## Typography and layout

- Use Manrope for interface and body text.
- Pair confident headings with comfortable body line-height and compact
  dashboard labels.
- Use generous spacing, subtle borders, restrained shadows, and 16–24px corners
  on major cards and composed surfaces.
- Keep marketplace and dashboard information scan-friendly without compressing
  key harvest dates, quantities, or statuses.

## Components and interaction

- Make primary actions clear, rounded, and easy to identify.
- Use quick, subtle hover and focus feedback; keep motion purposeful.
- Use green for primary actions and positive states. Do not use muted gold as a
  routine action color.
- Keep traceability, availability, harvest timing, and status information
  visually distinct.

## Product language

- Lead with “Demand before harvest” when explaining the product’s difference.
- Use “FarmFlow Harvest Intelligence” and explain the signals behind a
  recommendation.
- Keep language direct, trustworthy, and centered on local freshness and
  farmer confidence.
- Explain forecast confidence and limitations; do not claim scientific
  certainty.
- Do not reduce FarmFlow to a generic marketplace or refer to the feature only
  as “AI”.

## Motion

- Keep animation subtle, fast, and purposeful.
- Use motion to clarify the relationship between demand and harvest or to
  confirm a user action.
- Never over-animate, and never make essential information depend on motion.
